# Part A — Prompt task

## AI tool used

ChatGPT, GPT-5.5 Thinking

## Why I picked this tool

I picked ChatGPT because it is strong at writing SEO copy with specific constraints. It also the best ai in the world for now

## Prompt(s) I used

> You are helping with SEO copy for a fictional coffee brand called Atlas Coffee Tangier.
>
> The brand is a warm, premium but grounded specialty coffee shop in Tangier, Morocco. It focuses on single-origin beans, small-batch roasting, handcrafted coffee, and a medina-inspired atmosphere.
>
> I need 3 different H1 + meta description variations for a new product page for the brand’s signature blend.
>
> Constraints:
> - Target keyword: "specialty coffee tangier"
> - Each H1 should read like a real product page H1, not like a stuffed SEO tag.
> - Each H1 should be around 50–65 characters if possible.
> - Each meta description must be 150–160 characters maximum.
> - The keyword should appear naturally in at least the H1 of each variation, ideally also in the meta description.
> - Do not make the 3 variations near-identical. Use different angles: provenance, craft, and location.
> - Output only the 3 variations in a numbered list.
> - For each variation, include:
>   - H1
>   - Meta description

## Raw output from the AI (all 3 variations, unedited)

1. **H1:** Specialty Coffee Tangier with Single-Origin Depth  
   **Meta:** Discover Atlas Coffee Tangier’s signature blend: specialty coffee Tangier flavor, single-origin character, and a warm medina-inspired cup.

2. **H1:** Specialty Coffee Tangier, Slow-Roasted by Hand  
   **Meta:** Taste a slow-roasted signature blend from Atlas Coffee Tangier, crafted for specialty coffee Tangier lovers who value balance and depth.

3. **H1:** Specialty Coffee Tangier from the Heart of the Medina  
   **Meta:** Bring the Tangier medina into your cup with our signature specialty coffee Tangier blend, made for smooth mornings and handcrafted moments.

## Final pick + 2-line justification

**Picked variation: #3**

This is the strongest option because it connects the product to Tangier and the medina, which fits the Atlas Coffee brand voice.  
It also uses the target keyword naturally while sounding more emotional and less generic than the other variations.
